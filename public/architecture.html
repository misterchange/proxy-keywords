Seed Keywords Sheet Ingestion

What happens here:

The user feeds an Excel workbook or CSV list containing seed queries (e.g., "protein powder", "lyric generator") across target country codes.

Why this step matters:

Keyword matrices require comparisons across multiple regional markets. The system maps regional country codes (us, uk, in, etc.) and batches seeds into structured queues.

Key Anti-Bot Defense in this step:

Pacing starts here: Seeds are chunked into batches with configurable limits and start indexes to prevent high-frequency IP bursts.
